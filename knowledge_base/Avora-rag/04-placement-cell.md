# Training and Placement Cell (TPC)
_Last updated: 2026-09-27._

## Overview
The TPC bridges academic learning and employment, coordinating with corporate recruiters
year-round. It runs webinars, seminars, workshops, guest lectures and personality
development programs; conducts pre-placement training, competitive tests and group
discussions; and maintains an industry network for internships and full-time roles.
Related: the university's Awadh Incubation Foundation supports entrepreneurship (see
05-facilities-and-infrastructure.md).
