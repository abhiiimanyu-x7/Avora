# Pathshala ,  community outreach programme
_Last updated: 2026-09-27._

## Overview
KMCLU runs an after-hours Pathshala for underprivileged children living near campus, led
by Dr. Abhay Krishna (Programme Officer, NSS Unit-IV) with NSS volunteer support. It offers
academic support and guidance, including Navodaya Vidyalaya entrance-exam prep, general
studies, and sports. Volunteers also do outreach visits to nearby villages on the
importance of education and related social issues.

## What it does
- Free/supplementary education for children who can't access private schooling.
- Builds literacy, soft skills, hygiene awareness and basic moral education.
- Gives NSS student-volunteers leadership, teaching and communication experience.
- Strengthens the university's tie to the local community.

## Reported outcomes
Improved literacy and school performance among participants, higher school enrollment/
retention, more confident and disciplined children, stronger community-education awareness,
and leadership growth among the NSS volunteers who run it. Contributes to UN SDG goals on
quality education and reduced inequality.
