# Media Studio ,  detail
_Last updated: 2026-09-27. Supplements the brief mention in
06-infrastructure-and-facilities.md. Located in the Department of Journalism and Mass
Communication._

## Purpose
Integrates classroom instruction with hands-on media production ,  digital storytelling,
journalism, multimedia communication.

## Equipment and infrastructure
- Professional video cameras and audio equipment
- Soundproof recording booths (voiceovers, podcasts, narration)
- Dedicated mixing and audio production lab
- Green screen + soft-light ceiling rail channels for chroma keying and lighting control
- Editing suites running Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, Audacity and other
  industry-standard software
- Graphic design and visual-effects (GFX) unit
- Teleprompters, studio microphones, lighting grids

## What students do here
Scripting, anchoring, camera operation, editing and post-production; live news bulletins,
interviews, documentary shoots, and student-led productions, under faculty/media
professional guidance. Regular workshops and interdisciplinary collaborations cover
television, digital platforms, radio and social media production skills.
