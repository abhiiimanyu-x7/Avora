# Library membership and rules
_Last updated: 2026-09-27. Source: Ram Prasad Bismil Library circular._

## Eligibility
Students, research scholars, faculty and staff of KMCLU. Apply via the official library
membership application form.

## Visitor access
- Research scholars: need a recommendation letter from a supervisor or department.
- Visiting scholars: need valid photo ID plus a university-issued ID card.

## Borrowing privileges
| Category | Max books | Borrowing period |
|---|---|---|
| All teaching staff | 5 | 180 days |
| Guest faculty and staff | 3 | 30 days |
| Students | 3 | 14 days |

## Overdue charges
| Category | Charge |
|---|---|
| Teaching staff | ₹20/book/day |
| Guest faculty and staff | ₹15/book/day |
| Students | ₹1/book/day |

## Loss of borrowed books
Member must either replace with the same/current edition, or pay the book's current cost
(at prevailing exchange rate if imported). Additionally:
- ₹50 penalty applies.
- Overdue charges keep accruing until settlement.
- Payment goes to the university's designated account at a nationalized bank.
- Submit the payment counterfoil + book's Accession Number to the University Librarian.

## No Dues Certificate (end of course)
Students must return the library card and all borrowed books, then submit the printed
No-Dues application form. Retaining the card after course completion is treated as unlawful
and continues to accrue overdue charges.

## Library timings
- Library hours: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM (closed Sundays and holidays)
- Circulation counter: 10:30 AM - 3:30 PM (working days)
- Staff lunch break: 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM

## General rules
- Sign the entry register and show ID at entrance.
- Maintain silence inside the library.
- Deposit personal belongings at the property counter (token given).
- No mobile phone use inside.
- No food or drinks inside.
- Membership cards are non-transferable.
- Personal books/printed material not allowed inside.
- Marking, damaging or stealing library material triggers disciplinary action.
- Reading areas are for individual study ,  don't disturb others.
- Don't rest feet on furniture.
- Library is not responsible for lost/damaged personal belongings.
- Staff will not relay phone messages to readers.
- The Librarian may suspend a membership for misbehavior with staff or other members.
- Rules may be revised without prior notice ,  treat this file as a reference, not a
  guarantee, and point students to the library desk for the current version if it matters.
