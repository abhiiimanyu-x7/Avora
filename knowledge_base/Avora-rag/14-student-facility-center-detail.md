# Student Facility Center (SFC) ,  detail
_Last updated: 2026-09-27. Supplements the brief SFC entry in
06-infrastructure-and-facilities.md with fuller detail._

## Office of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS)
Houses the IKS office, a Ministry of Education (Govt. of India) initiative for preserving
and disseminating India's traditional knowledge heritage. Supports interdisciplinary
research across arts, literature, sciences, technology and agriculture.

## Canteen
On-site canteen with nutritious, hygienic, reasonably priced food; a social space for
students and staff to relax and talk between classes.

## Bank extension center
ICICI Bank branch inside the SFC for account management and transactions on campus (see
also 24x7 ATM noted in 06-infrastructure-and-facilities.md).

## Art gallery
Exhibits student, faculty and visiting-artist work; hosts exhibitions, workshops and
cultural programmes. Themes include social awareness, women's empowerment, environmental
consciousness, Indian heritage and contemporary issues.

## Museum
Shares space conceptually with the campus museum described in
06-infrastructure-and-facilities.md ,  historical artifacts (coins, manuscripts, sculptures,
weapons, paintings) and images of Sanchi, Sarnath, Brihadeshwar, Konark, and Ajanta/Ellora
cave art.

## Also inside the SFC (from the original facilities list)
Print/photocopy shop, stationery shop, open theater, common room + hobby room for girls,
NCC and NSS offices.
