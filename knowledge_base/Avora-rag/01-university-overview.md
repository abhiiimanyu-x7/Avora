# University overview
_Last updated: 2026-09-27. Source: KMCLU official pages + Prospectus 2026-27._

## Identity
Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti Language University (KMCLU), Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, is a State
Public multidisciplinary and multilingual university focused on languages, humanities,
social sciences, professional education, and emerging technologies. It integrates
linguistic scholarship with interdisciplinary, skill-based learning aligned to NEP 2020.

## Key facts
- Established under the Uttar Pradesh Universities Act, 2009.
- Renamed Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti Language University in 2020 (expanded linguistic mandate).
- Recognized under UGC Sections 2(f) and 12(B).
- First academic session began August 2013 (UG, PG, Diploma programmes).
- Student strength exceeds 5,000 across 70+ programmes (2026-27 session: 98 courses across
  departments per the Vice Chancellor's prospectus message).
- Received first NAAC accreditation in 2025 (reported as CGPA 2.86 / B++ Grade in different
  official sources ,  confirm current grade before quoting).
- Location: Sitapur-Hardoi Bypass Road, Lucknow - 226013.
- Campus Wi-Fi: 1 Gbps network campus-wide.

## Programme expansion timeline
- 2019: AICTE-approved B.Tech in Civil, Mechanical, Computer Science, and CSE (AI & ML).
- 2021-22: LL.M. (Faculty of Legal Studies), M.Tech (Faculty of Engineering and Technology).
- 2022-23: LLB, BA.LLB introduced; Faculty of Pharmacy established (B.Pharm, D.Pharm).
- 2023: Faculty of Pharmacy formally established under PCI approval, intake 60 each for
  B.Pharm and D.Pharm.
- Recent: U.G. course in Awadhi language introduced.

## Vision
To achieve excellence in language, education, research and innovation that grooms learners
into productive, responsible, ethical, creative and compassionate members of society.

## Mission
- Build a culture of inclusion, respect and collaboration among faculty, students and staff.
- Explore Indian and foreign languages while preserving the Indian Knowledge System, using
  modern technology.
- Deliver a transformative educational experience centered on value-based learning,
  transparent ethics, community engagement, and participative leadership.
- Provide access to diverse populations and global opportunities, preparing students to lead
  in language, research, innovation, critical thinking and nation-building.

## Research and innovation
- IoT and Artificial Intelligence Networking Laboratories support applied research in
  Machine Learning and Data Science.
- Awadh Incubation Foundation (AIF) supports student/alumni startups (see
  03-administration-contacts.md and 07-facilities.md for details).
- Structured commitment to UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
- Awadh Shodh Peeth: research and cultural initiative on Awadh region heritage, history,
  language, and folklore.
- Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyay Shodhpeeth: established by UP State Government in 2017, focused on
  his life, thought and philosophy.
