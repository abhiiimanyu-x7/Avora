# Ashfaq Ullah Khan University Guest House
_Last updated: 2026-09-27._

## Purpose
On-campus lodging for parents/guardians of students, visiting faculty, guest lecturers and
official invitees, at nominal university-approved rates.

## Facilities
Furnished rooms, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, private ensuite bathrooms, RO drinking water,
green campus setting.

## Charges
| Room type | General charge | KMCLU faculty/staff charge |
|---|---|---|
| AC room | ₹900/night | ₹700/night |
| Non-AC room | ₹500/night | ₹300/night |

## Booking rules ,  faculty/non-academic staff
- Book for self or visiting guests at approved rates.
- Application (prescribed format) submitted at least 2 working days before arrival.
- Allotment: first-come, first-served.
- Max 2 people per room; no extra bed requests.

## Booking rules ,  students (for visiting parents/guardians)
- Requires recommendation from the respective Hostel Warden.
- Application at least 2 working days prior to arrival.
- Allotment normally for a maximum of 2 days, first-come first-served, no advance booking.
- Only one room at concessional (parent) rate; extra rooms for extra family members charged
  at normal rate.
- Max 2 people per room; no extra bed requests.
- Must submit Aadhar card of the person for whom the room is booked.

## Where to apply
Administrative Block, Deputy Registrar Office. Email: guesthouse@kmclu.ac.in
