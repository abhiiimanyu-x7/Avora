# Infrastructure and campus facilities
_Last updated: 2026-09-27._

## Abul Kalam Azad Academic Block
Classrooms with projectors, auditorium, faculty rooms, seminar/conference halls, and a
dedicated reading room on every floor.

## Laboratories
Computer labs in every department; language labs for Arabic, Persian, Urdu, English,
French; Chemistry, Physics, Electrical, Electronics, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, Civil and
Mechanical labs/workshops; a Media Lab; Faculty of Science labs for Zoology, Biotechnology,
Microbiology, Botany, Chemistry.

## Acharya Narendra Deva Administrative Building
Houses the offices of the Vice Chancellor, Finance Officer, Controller of Examinations and
Registrar; manages student affairs and financial matters.

## Students' Facility Centre (SFC)
Operating since 2021-22. Canteen, print/photocopy shop, stationery shop, ICICI Bank branch
with 24x7 ATM, open theater, common room and hobby room for girls, NCC and NSS offices.

## Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi Study Centre
Houses Department of Home Science, Department of Journalism and Mass Communication, and
Department of English & Modern European and Asian Languages.

## Community Health Centre
MoUs with ERA University, Career Institute of Medical/Dental Sciences (IIM Road), and
Balrampur Hospital (Kaiserbagh) for OPD, dental and hospital services at nominal fees,
including ambulance service. Runs a 24-hour OPD; nurse available round the clock, doctor
available 4 hours/day.

## Ram Prasad Bismil Library
Extensive book collection (5,000+ in Arts and Humanities alone), periodicals, magazines,
journals, and online resources. E-Library portal built with Knimbus for digital books,
journals and databases.

## Chandrashekhar Azad Gymnasium
Exercise equipment, table tennis, badminton courts, Yoga Centre. Also: an Open Gym in an
outdoor setting, and a basketball court + cricket net-practice facility.

## Media Studio
In the Department of Journalism and Mass Communication. HD indoor/outdoor cameras,
teleprompter, interactive screens, editing system, dedicated audio section for voiceovers
and radio training.

## Museum
Preserves historical objects and cultural heritage: ancient coins, manuscripts, sculptures,
weapons, paintings; images of Sanchi, Sarnath, Brihadeshwar, Konark, Ajanta/Ellora cave art.

## Moot Court
Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Memorial Moot Court Hall (Faculty of Legal Studies). Compulsory component
of B.A. LL.B. and LL.B. curricula.

## Greenhouse, Herbal Garden, Yoga Vatika
- Greenhouse: controlled-environment cultivation/research for Botany, Zoology,
  Environmental Science, Agriculture, Biotechnology.
- Herbal Garden: medicinal/aromatic plants, scientifically identified and bar-coded, used
  by Life Sciences, Environmental Studies, Pharmacy.
- Yoga Vatika: green space for Yoga, Pranayama, Meditation and wellness activities.

## Sustainability infrastructure
- Rainwater Harvesting System (water conservation, groundwater recharge).
- Solar Power Plant: 150kW on library rooftop, another 150kW planned on academic block;
  1000kW plant planned campus-wide.

## Auditorium (under construction)
1,200-seat capacity auditorium being built by UP Rajkiya Nirman Nigam Limited.

## Awadh Incubation Foundation (AIF)
A Section 8 (not-for-profit) company at KMCLU, guided by a Board of Directors. Open to all
students/alumni and outside entrepreneurs with a launch-ready idea. Provides office space,
business advisory, and incubation support. Website: www.aiflucknow.in

## Women Study Centre, Lifts/Ramps, NEP 2020, e-Learning
See 03-committees-and-student-support.md for Women Study Centre.
Accessibility: lifts and ramps being added to the academic building (3 lifts functional as
of the latest prospectus).
NEP 2020: multidisciplinary curriculum, skill-based learning, multiple entry/exit options.
e-Learning: SWAYAM-NPTEL Local Chapter (LC ID: 6845); 500+ video lectures on the official
YouTube channel; C.M. Abhyudaya Yojana for Civil Services coaching.

## Bank and ATM
ICICI Bank branch on campus with 24x7 ATM.
