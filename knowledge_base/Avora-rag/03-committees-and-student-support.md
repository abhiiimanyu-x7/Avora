## Internal Complaints Committee
Handles sexual-harassment complaints for women at the university, per NCW guidelines and
the Vishaka judgment (Supreme Court, 13 Aug 1997). Aims for a safe, confident campus for
every woman.

## Students' Grievances and Redressal Cell (SGRC)
Set up per UGC guidelines; a Lokpal is nominated for transparent, accountable grievance
handling. Detailed guidelines on the university website.

## Anti-Ragging Committee
Advisory/supervisory body for a ragging-free campus. Conducts inspections and orientation
sessions on ragging's consequences. Coordinated by the Proctor.

## Gender Sensitization Committee
Works on equality in law, social systems and democratic participation for all stakeholders.
