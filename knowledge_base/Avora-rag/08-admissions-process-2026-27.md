## Application fee
- General & OBC: ₹500
- SC/ST: ₹250
- Diploma programmes: ₹100

## How to apply
1. Register online at https://kmcluadm.samarth.edu.in/ ,  no other mode accepted.
2. Fill personal details, get a system-generated application number + set a password.
3. Complete educational qualification details, programme selection, upload photo/signature
   (JPG/JPEG, photo up to 1MB, 80% face visible without mask) and category certificates.
4. Pay the fee online (Net Banking / Credit / Debit / UPI). Fee is non-refundable.
5. Download and keep the Confirmation Page and the completed Application Form.

## Important rules students should know
- One application form per candidate; one mobile number and email per form, and they must
  be the candidate's own (or a parent/guardian's) ,  used for all official communication.
- No changes allowed to submitted details (name, DOB, category, etc.) after submission.
- Appearing for the entrance test does not guarantee admission ,  programme-wise eligibility
  must still be met.
- Exams may run in multiple shifts; no request for shift/date change is entertained.
- Admit card + valid photo ID required to enter the exam hall; report 1 hour early.
- Working candidates cannot pursue a full-time programme without an employer's leave
  certificate submitted within 1 month of admission.
- Furnishing false information leads to cancellation of candidature/result and possible
  legal action under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).
- Ragging is a punishable offence; admitted students must submit an anti-ragging
  undertaking.
- Attendance requirement: minimum 75% for lectures/tutorials and separately 75% for
  practicals to sit the semester-end exam (up to 15% relaxation possible for serious
  illness/approved co-curricular participation, with a supporting medical certificate).

## Documents required at admission (originals + self-attested photocopies)
1. High School marksheet & certificate
2. Intermediate/equivalent marksheet & certificate (+ UG degree for PG programmes)
3. Transfer/Migration Certificate (original at counselling)
4. Character Certificate from last institution head or a Gazetted Officer
5. EWS/Caste certificate (if claiming reserved-category seat)
6. Any other certificate supporting a reservation/weightage claim
7. Prescribed fee
8. Gap affidavit (if applicable)

## Foreign national / NRI admissions
Same eligibility as Indian students per programme; must hold qualifications recognized
equivalent by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU); visa documents required within
1 month of admission completion.

## Fee refund policy
- Application fee: non-refundable.
- Other charges: refundable only if admission is cancelled within 1 month or by the
  admission deadline, whichever is earlier (excludes admission/enrollment fee).
- Caution money: refunded only after final results are declared; forfeited if unclaimed
  for 3+ years.
- Unclaimed credit balances: forfeited after 1 year.

## Admission via Madarsa certificates (UG level)
Madarsa certificates accepted at AMU Aligarh, Jamia Millia Islamia, Lucknow University,
University of Delhi, or Maulana Azad National Urdu University are also accepted at KMCLU
for the 2026-27 session.

## Disputes and jurisdiction
All admission disputes go to the Admission Committee / Vice Chancellor (decision final).
Legal matters are subject to Lucknow jurisdiction only.
